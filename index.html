The review rhythm

Make time
to look back.

A quick conversation can change next week’s plan. A longer review helps you see what’s shifted over a term or a year.

  1. Weekly

    Check in

    Ask what felt good, what was hard, and what support would help next week.

  2. Monthly

    Reset priorities

    Review goals and habits. Choose what to keep, adjust, or let go.

  3. Quarterly

    Look for patterns

    Step back across the ten life areas and choose where to focus next.

  4. Annually

    Tell the story

    Gather the milestones and observations that capture who your child was this year.

For parents
