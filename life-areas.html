One family example

Follow one interest
across several areas.

A child who wants to grow a tomato plant can learn how plants use light, test two growing spots, and draw what changes each week. One project gives you something to notice in Knowledge, Cognitive, and Creativity.

Pick a focus

Ask which part they’re most excited about. Choose one small action they can repeat, such as checking the plant and sketching it every Saturday.

Review together

At the end of the month, ask what surprised them. Keep a favourite sketch or observation and let their answer shape the next goal.
