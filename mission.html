Our mission

Orchard is a digital platform for paying attention to how your child is growing, supporting what they need now, and keeping the moments that tell the story of their childhood.

Why we built Orchard

Make time to notice.

School deadlines and appointments are easy to see. A growing interest, a change in confidence, or a small act of courage can be easier to miss.

Explore the principles

Why the name Orchard?

An orchard is tended over seasons. We chose the name because childhood asks for that same steady attention. Some seasons call for a new goal. Others call for space, rest, or a conversation.

Orchard brings that attention into everyday family life. Review what’s changed, let your child help decide what comes next, and make a little room for all ten areas of growth.

Four questions
we build around.

These questions connect the way Orchard approaches goals, habits, check-ins, and family records.

Who are they becoming?

Use goals and reviews to notice the interests, values, and strengths your child is developing.

What do they need right now?

Talk through what’s going well and what feels hard. Look for patterns together before choosing how to help.

What have we noticed?

Keep the details you want to remember, from the questions they kept asking to the day something finally clicked.

What will they carry forward?

Give them chances to practice making choices and a record of the experiences, values, and people that shaped them.

Give them their story.

A favourite book, a first act of courage, a letter you wrote after a milestone. Orchard connects those details across years so they can become something your child can return to as an adult.

The Legacy Letter brings that record together as a gift at eighteen, in your own words.
